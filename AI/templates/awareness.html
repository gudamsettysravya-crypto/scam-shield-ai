Scam Awareness Center

Educate yourself about common scam tactics used in India. Click any card to learn more. Knowledge is your first line of defence.

🎁 Prize / Lottery Scams
High Risk

What is it: Fraudsters claim you won a huge prize, car, or lottery, demanding an advance "processing fee" or tax upfront.

Warning Signs to Watch For:

  • Asks for upfront registration or release fee
  • Urgent deadline: "Claim within 24 hours"
  • Links to suspicious domains (.xyz, .top)

🏦 Banking / Phishing Scams
Critical

What is it: Impersonating banks, NPCI, or RBI to steal OTPs, passwords, or net-banking credentials via SMS and calls.

Warning Signs to Watch For:

  • Threatens immediate account suspension
  • Link contains raw IP or fake portal
  • Demands OTP or PIN verbally
Read Complete Guide
💼 Fake Job Scams
Prevalent

What is it: Part-time work-from-home offers promising unrealistic earnings for liking videos, always demanding fees first.

Warning Signs to Watch For:

  • Unrealistic income (₹3000/day for simple tasks)
  • Requires upfront registration fee or deposit
  • Recruitment strictly via Telegram/WhatsApp
Read Complete Guide
💸 UPI / Payment Scams
Financial

What is it: Scammers send QR codes claiming "scan to receive money", but scanning and entering PIN debits your account.

Warning Signs to Watch For:

  • Collect request disguised as payment receipt
  • Stranger sends QR code claiming to send funds
  • Fake screenshot of payment transfer
Read Complete Guide
💕 Romance Scams
Social

What is it: Fake online relationships on social media or matrimony apps that build trust before demanding emergency funds.

Warning Signs to Watch For:

  • Love-bombing and rapid emotional bonding
  • Avoids video calls or in-person meetings
  • Financial crisis: medical, gift stuck at customs
Read Complete Guide
🔗 Malicious URL Scams
Cyber

What is it: Links disguised as legitimate brands using look-alike domains, URL shorteners, or unencrypted HTTP channels.

Warning Signs to Watch For:

  • Slightly misspelled domain names
  • Suspicious TLDs (.xyz, .tk, .top, .online)
  • Unencrypted HTTP links without SSL

Golden Rules to Stay Safe

Never Share OTPs

No bank, government, or NPCI official will ever ask you for your OTP over the phone or message.

Never Pay to Get Paid

Legitimate prizes, job offers, or lottery wins never require an upfront fee to process or release the reward.

Verify Before You Click

Always check the full URL domain before clicking. Use SCAMSHIELD AI to analyze suspicious links.

Report Cybercrime

File complaints at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 (National Cyber Helpline).
