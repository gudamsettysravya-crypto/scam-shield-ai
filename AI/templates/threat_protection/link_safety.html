Inspect a Suspicious Link

How Scammers Disguise URLs
1. Typo-Squatting / Lookalikes Replacing letters with similar characters, e.g. amaz0n.com or sbi-update.site.
2. Subdomain Deception Placing the brand name as a prefix, e.g. sbi.com.attacker-domain.xyz.
3. Unencrypted HTTP Legitimate banking and payment portals ALWAYS use SSL (HTTPS) with a verified certificate.
