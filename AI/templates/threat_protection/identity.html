Personal Identity & Credential Protection

Comprehensive security guidelines to safeguard your most sensitive digital assets: OTPs, passwords, bank accounts, UPI PINs, Aadhaar/PAN, and private identity information.

1. OTP Protection

One-Time Passwords are your final transaction authorization layer. Once shared, the financial transfer is irreversible.

  • Read the purpose: Always check whether the SMS says "transferring Rs. X" or "logging into account".
  • Zero voice disclosure: Never speak OTP digits to anyone claiming to be a bank manager, courier, or telecom executive.
  • Unsolicited OTPs: If you receive an OTP without requesting it, someone may have your password; change it immediately.
2. Passwords & Logins

Strong authentication hygiene prevents credential stuffing and brute-force attacks across your accounts.

  • Use unique passphrases: At least 14 characters combining uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
  • No password recycling: Never reuse your email or primary banking password on other shopping websites.
  • Enable 2FA everywhere: Use authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Aegis) instead of SMS 2FA when available.
3. Bank & Card Details

Your account numbers, CVVs, expiry dates, and net-banking credentials require strict operational secrecy.

  • Never share CVV: The 3-digit CVV on the back of your card must never be photographed or sent via chat.
  • Set daily transaction limits: Keep online debit card limits low and enable international usage only when traveling.
  • Direct address entry: Always type your bank's URL directly (e.g. onlinesbi.sbi) rather than clicking search engine ad links.
4. UPI & Payment Safety

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) transfers execute instantaneously and cannot be canceled once authorized.

  • Receiving does NOT need PIN: Entering your UPI PIN always DEDUCTS money. You NEVER enter a PIN to get money.
  • Never scan stranger QR codes: "Scan to accept ₹5,000 refund" is 100% a fraudulent debit trick.
  • Decline unrequested collect calls: Always reject sudden collect requests from unknown VPA handles in GPay/PhonePe.
5. Aadhaar & PAN Security

Government IDs can be exploited for synthetic identity theft, unauthorized SIM card activations, or illegal loan apps.

  • Use Masked Aadhaar: Download the official masked Aadhaar from UIDAI where only the last 4 digits are visible.
  • Lock your Biometrics: Use the mAadhaar app to lock your biometric data; unlock it only during legitimate KYC.
  • Never share ID on WhatsApp: Do not send photocopies of PAN/Aadhaar to unverified job recruiters or loan agents.
6. Personal Information Privacy

Scammers aggregate social media clues (birthdays, family names, travel plans) to build hyper-convincing spear phishing attacks.

  • Don't post travel tickets: Flight boarding passes contain PNR barcodes that expose personal passport details.
  • Set social accounts to private: Restrict stranger access to your family contacts and workplace history.
  • Beware of impersonation calls: Fraudsters use AI voice clones; establish a secret family safeword.
